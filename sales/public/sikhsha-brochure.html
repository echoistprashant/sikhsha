Everything a school office runs on, finally in one place.

Sikhsha ERP handles the daily grind of school administration — admissions, attendance, fees, communication — so principals and admins spend less time on paperwork and more time on outcomes.

👤
Student Records & Admissions

Complete student profiles — guardian info, documents, roll numbers, class history, and promotions — in one searchable system. Never lose a record again.

📅
Attendance Management

Mark daily, subject-wise, or class-wise attendance from supported workspaces. Generate clean summaries, spot patterns, and export reports quickly.

💳
Fee Collection & Receipts

Generate invoices, collect payments online via Razorpay or offline, issue PDF receipts, track refunds, and identify defaulters before they pile up.

🔔
Parent Communication

Send announcements, absence notices, assignment updates, and learning resources directly to parents — all tied to the student's operational record.

📊
Management Dashboard

Live view of fee collections, attendance rates, open dues, and activity logs. School owners get a command center instead of a stack of printed reports.

Role-Based Access

Separate, permission-controlled workspaces for Admin, Teacher, Student, and Parent. Everyone sees exactly what they need — nothing more.

Part 2 — AI Classroom Tools

AI that helps teachers teach and students learn — faster.

Beyond the ERP, Sikhsha EDU brings AI into the classroom workflow. Teachers prepare better, students understand faster, and management gets real learning signals.

⭐ Key Feature
❓

AI Question Generator

Generate topic-specific questions quickly. Choose subject, grade, chapter, difficulty level, and question type (MCQ, short answer, descriptive) to support teacher preparation.

⭐ Key Feature
📝

Smart Quiz Engine

Assign auto-generated quizzes to students with one click. Students attempt quizzes on the app, get instant scores and feedback, and teachers see who needs help — without manual checking.

⭐ Key Feature
🖼️

AI Teaching Deck Builder

Input topic, grade, subject, and slide count. Create a structured teaching deck with content, activities, and assessments so teachers can focus more on delivery.
